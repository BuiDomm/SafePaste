# Code of Conduct

We are committed to a harassment-free, welcoming community for everyone, regardless of background, identity, experience, or ability.

Be respectful, assume good faith, discuss technical tradeoffs with evidence, and protect the privacy of users and contributors. Harassment, threats, doxxing, publication of private data, and discriminatory conduct are unacceptable.

Project maintainers may edit or remove contributions and restrict participation when behavior threatens people, the community, or the security of the project. Report conduct concerns privately to the maintainers. Reports will be reviewed promptly and handled with appropriate confidentiality.
